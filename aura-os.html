Built from scratch

Aura OS

A custom operating system built to make AI music the way I hear it in my head — not the way a template wants me to.

The system

I didn't use someone else's tools.
I built mine.

Aura OS is the custom AI workflow system I built to create Forever Still Music. It's not a DAW. It's not a plugin. It's a purpose-built environment that handles everything from emotional prompt engineering to visual generation — all tuned to my voice, my face, and my story.

Every song on Harder Than the Army ran through Aura. Every visual you've seen of Forever Still was generated inside it. 32,000+ collective plays on SoundCloud. Zero ads. Zero label. One system.

I'm a U.S. Army veteran and solo mom of two. I didn't have a studio budget or a team. I had time, obsession, and a problem I needed to solve. Aura OS is what happened when I refused to settle for someone else's answer.

"AI didn't make me an artist. It gave me a system powerful enough to keep up with what I already was."

What Aura OS does

Every part of the music is intentional.

01 — Sound
AI Music Generation

Custom prompt architecture tuned to my emotional vocabulary. Not generic. Not random. Aura produces the specific feeling I'm after — grief, grit, stillness, defiance.

02 — Visuals
LoRA-Trained on My Face

I trained a LoRA model on my own likeness so the visual identity of Forever Still Music is actually me — not a stock avatar or AI-generic face. Every image is mine.

03 — Workflow
End-to-End Production

From lyrics to audio to art to release — Aura OS connects every step. Built for a solo operator who needs a full production pipeline without a full production team.

04 — Story
Emotional Prompt Engineering

The hardest part of AI music isn't the tool — it's knowing what to ask for. Aura is built around a method for translating lived experience into prompts that produce real feeling.

05 — Identity
Brand-Consistent Output

Every output — audio, visual, copy — runs through a brand layer that keeps Forever Still cohesive. Dark. Gold. Honest. That's not a mood board. It's a constraint baked into the system.

06 — Scale
Built for One. Scales to Many.

The custom song service on this site — Your Life, Your Song — runs on Aura. Your story goes in. A real song comes out.

Why this exists

The music industry didn't have a seat for me.
So I built the table.

I'm not a tech founder. I'm not a music executive. I'm a veteran who came home, raised two boys alone, survived things I don't list on websites, and found that the only way to say what I needed to say was to build something that didn't exist yet.

Aura OS is proof that you don't need a label, a studio, or a team. You need a story worth telling and the willingness to figure out how to tell it. I figured it out. Then I built it into a system. Then I used that system to make an album that's touched 32,000+ people with no advertising.

That's what Aura does. That's what it's for.
